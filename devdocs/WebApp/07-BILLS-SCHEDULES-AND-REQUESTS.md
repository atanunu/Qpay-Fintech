# Household bills, calendars and requests

Saved bills have an owner, nickname, encrypted customer reference, product and optional usual amount/favourite. Validate and quote afresh before purchase. The token archive retrieves the original protected value. Timestamped availability may be unknown; watching is not a guarantee.

Reminders never debit. Internal mandates require explicit PIN/MFA-approved immutable terms, recurrence/end dates, occurrence limits and per-occurrence/total caps. Every occurrence is uniquely identified and rechecks policy/funds. Unsafe execution pauses; fresh approval is required after pause. External autopay is not implemented.

Request shares must sum exactly. Partial payments are scoped to the authenticated payer. Recheck expiry, cancellation and unpaid amount in the ledger transaction. QR/links open review, not debit approval.
