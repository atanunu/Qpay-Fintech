# Growth and partner-product gates

The approved chain remains WebApp/MobileApp → APIbackend → QPay and approved partners. Never put upstream credentials in a browser. Everyday-customer parity does not activate every competitor product.

Cards, interest savings, lending, contribution groups, business banking, international payments, investments and insurance remain separate programmes. Goal pockets, funded rewards and the read-only assistant are not completed. Before enablement record eligibility, operating/custody arrangements, economics, disclosures, accounting, fraud controls, support and partner acceptance. API access is not financial operating permission.
