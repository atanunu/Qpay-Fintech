# Provider procurement and commercial readiness
Status: provider shortlist and evaluation process, not endorsements or approved integrations.

Prefer first auditing QPay's actual supported APIs rather than signing duplicate upstream integrations blindly. Required categories are account/custody sponsor, bank-transfer execution, collections/virtual accounts, bills, identity verification and notifications. Optional cards/FX/credit require separate decisions.

## Provider acceptance matrix
Every provider/capability needs a record containing legal entity; current licence/partner model; contracted functionality; country/currency coverage; onboarding obligations; settlement account structure; safeguarding responsibilities; API version and SDK support; credentials/environment; signatures; idempotency and original-reference queries; timeout/pending rules; reversals/returns; bill validation/token recovery; statement/reconciliation format; uptime and support escalation; prefunding; price schedule; taxes/fees; chargebacks/loss responsibility; data processing; sandbox evidence; production acceptance and approver.

Use actual contract quotes, not assumed free calls, advertised percentages or historical prices. A cheap vend cost can be outweighed by poor pending-resolution support and reconciliation gaps. Require independent operational access to statements rather than only callback notifications.

## Commercial dashboards to implement
Customer fee and partner commission by product; cost and margin by upstream; unrecovered fees; refund economics; prefunded balances and thresholds; merchant settlement exposure; aged unresolved payments; fraud losses; reward liabilities; support cost per transaction; KYC conversion; bill fulfilment success; revenue and cash separately.

Never fund operating expenses from safeguarded customer balances. Approve customer terms, displayed fees, refunds, complaint escalation and service-availability messaging before launch. Exact amounts, limits, fee/tax policies and service commitments remain configurable and subject to review.
